# Grolo Legal & Compliance Pack

These are founder-friendly policy drafts for Grolo, a local marketplace connecting buyers and sellers. They are not legal advice and must be reviewed, adapted, and approved by qualified counsel before publication.

## Launch checklist

Before launch, replace every `[bracketed placeholder]`, confirm the legal entity and operating jurisdiction, and have counsel validate consumer, privacy, payments, tax, accessibility, marketing, and marketplace obligations in each launch location.

Publish the Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Community Guidelines, Acceptable Use Policy, Refund/Cancellation Policy, Marketplace Safety Policy, Copyright/DMCA Process, and Account Deletion Policy in the app and on the website. Link the Seller Agreement during seller onboarding and make the UGC Moderation Policy accessible from reporting and appeal flows.
