# Grolo Account Deletion Policy

**Effective date:** [DATE]

You may request deletion of your Grolo account from **[in-app path]** or by contacting **[account support email]**. We may verify ownership before acting.

## What deletion does

We will deactivate the account and delete or anonymize personal information that we are not required or permitted to retain, subject to technical and legal limits. We will stop ordinary account communications, except messages needed to complete deletion or required service/legal notices.

## What may remain

We may retain transaction, tax, fraud-prevention, safety, moderation, dispute, security, and legal records for **[retention period]** or longer where law requires. Content already shared with other users—such as reviews, public listing references, or messages in their records—may remain, be attributed to a non-identifying label, or be removed where feasible and lawful. Backups may persist until routine rotation.

## Pending activity

Before deletion, resolve or document open orders, refunds, reports, disputes, or legal holds where possible. Deletion does not cancel amounts owed, erase another person’s lawful records, or remove liability for conduct before deletion.

## Timing and appeal

We will acknowledge requests within **[X days]** and generally complete them within **[Y days]**, subject to verification, legal exceptions, and technical constraints. If we cannot fulfill a request, we will explain the reason and available appeal or complaint route where required.

## Reopening an account

After deletion, you may need to create a new account. Grolo may retain limited suppression or security data to prevent fraud, abuse, or evasion, as permitted by law.
