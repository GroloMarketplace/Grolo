# Grolo Community Guidelines

**Effective date:** [DATE]

Grolo works when people treat one another honestly and locally. These guidelines apply to listings, profiles, messages, comments, reviews, promotions, and other community activity.

## Do

- Describe items, condition, quantity, price, location, and availability accurately.
- Communicate clearly and respectfully; use Grolo’s reporting and blocking tools.
- Meet in appropriate public places and follow the safety guidance.
- Leave honest, relevant reviews based on real transactions.
- Respect privacy, accessibility needs, and local laws.

## Do not

- Threaten, harass, stalk, bully, discriminate against, or sexually exploit anyone.
- Impersonate a person or business, evade a ban, manipulate reviews, or create deceptive listings.
- Post personal data, intimate images, doxxing material, or private conversations without lawful permission.
- Spam, scam, phish, distribute malware, or pressure users into unsafe payments.
- List prohibited, recalled, counterfeit, stolen, regulated, or unsafe goods.
- Use hate speech or content that promotes violence or exploitation.

We may remove content, warn, limit features, suspend, or terminate accounts. We may escalate credible threats, child-safety concerns, fraud, or other illegal activity to appropriate authorities. Reports are handled confidentially where practicable, but we cannot guarantee anonymity or a particular outcome. Submit a report in-app or at **[safety email]**.
