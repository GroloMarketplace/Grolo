# Grolo Copyright / DMCA Process

**Effective date:** [DATE]

Grolo respects intellectual-property rights. This process is designed for copyright complaints concerning listings, photos, videos, text, messages, reviews, or other content. It should be adapted by counsel for each country where Grolo operates.

## Copyright complaint

Send a notice to **[designated copyright email and postal address]** containing:

1. identification of the copyrighted work (or representative list);
2. identification and location/URL of the allegedly infringing content;
3. your name and contact information;
4. a good-faith statement that the use is not authorized by the owner, agent, or law;
5. a statement, under penalty of perjury where applicable, that the information is accurate and you are authorized to act; and
6. your physical or electronic signature.

Incomplete notices may delay review. We may forward the notice to the uploader where lawful and necessary.

## Review and action

Grolo may remove or restrict access to reported content, notify the account holder, request more information, and apply repeat-infringer or other enforcement measures. We do not decide complex ownership disputes and may preserve evidence.

## Counter-notice

Where applicable, the account holder may send a counter-notice identifying the removed material and location, contact information, a good-faith statement of mistake or misidentification, consent to the relevant court’s jurisdiction, and signature. Grolo may restore content as required by applicable law unless the complainant takes timely legal action.

## Misrepresentations

Knowingly false notices or counter-notices may create legal liability. This process does not limit other rights or remedies.
