# Grolo Marketplace Safety Policy

**Effective date:** [DATE]

Grolo promotes safer discovery, communication, and handoffs, but no online marketplace can eliminate all risk. Users remain responsible for reasonable precautions.

## Safer transactions

- Keep relevant communication in Grolo and verify the listing, person, and payment details.
- Do not share passwords, one-time codes, full financial credentials, or unnecessary identity documents.
- Prefer public, well-lit meeting locations; tell someone where you are going and use delivery precautions.
- Inspect goods before payment or handoff when practical. Do not enter a private location if you feel unsafe.
- Sellers should use secure packaging, disclose hazards, and follow recall and product-safety requirements.

## Prohibited safety risks

No weapons or unlawful controlled goods, stolen/counterfeit goods, recalled or materially unsafe products, exploitative services, trafficking, or listings that create a foreseeable serious risk may be listed. **[Counsel should tailor this list to launch jurisdictions and product categories.]**

## Reporting and response

Use Report, Block, or **[safety email/phone]** for threats, scams, unsafe goods, harassment, suspicious payments, or emergencies. Do not use Grolo for emergencies—contact local emergency services first. Grolo may hide content, restrict contact, suspend accounts, preserve records, notify affected users, or contact authorities. We may not disclose investigation details where doing so would create risk or violate privacy.

## Seller safety obligations

Sellers must maintain lawful inventory, accurate safety information, traceability and recall records where required, and cooperate with buyer, regulator, and Grolo requests. A seller must immediately report a serious incident, recall, or dangerous defect to **[safety contact]**.
