## 1. Information we collect

- **Account and profile:** name, email, phone number, profile photo, location or service area, authentication-provider identifier, and account preferences.
- **Marketplace activity:** listings, photos and descriptions, prices, availability, favorites, follows, promotions, reviews, ratings, reports, blocks, and notification settings.

- **Communications and content:** messages, comments, support requests, and other user-generated content.
- **Device and usage data:** IP address, device/browser information, approximate location, timestamps, crash data, and interactions with the Service.
- **Information from others:** reports, fraud or safety signals, referrals, and information provided by authentication, hosting, analytics, or payment partners.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information unless needed for a specific lawful purpose and disclosed to you.

We use information to provide accounts, listings, search, messaging, orders, notifications, promotions, favorites, follows, reviews, reporting, blocking, moderation, customer support, security, fraud prevention, analytics, service improvement, and legal compliance. We may send service messages; marketing messages will include an unsubscribe method where required by law.
