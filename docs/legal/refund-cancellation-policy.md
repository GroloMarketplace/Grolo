# Grolo Refund and Cancellation Policy

**Effective date:** [DATE]

This policy explains Grolo’s baseline approach to order cancellation and refunds. It does not remove mandatory consumer, cooling-off, withdrawal, product-safety, or other rights in the buyer’s location.

## 1. Before acceptance

A buyer may request cancellation before the seller accepts an order. The seller should promptly confirm cancellation. If Grolo has not processed payment, Grolo will not hold or return funds; the parties must follow the agreed cash/e-transfer arrangement.

## 2. After acceptance

A buyer may request cancellation before preparation or fulfillment begins. Sellers should accept where they can reasonably do so. Sellers may cancel when inventory is unavailable, a listing is materially inaccurate, safety requires it, or fulfillment is impossible, and should promptly explain and refund amounts received.

## 3. Non-conforming or missing items

A buyer should contact the seller promptly—normally within **[X days]**—with the order reference and evidence. The seller should offer an appropriate remedy, such as correction, replacement, partial refund, or full refund, where the item is materially different, damaged before handoff, missing, or not provided. Legal rights and seller warranties may require more.

## 4. Payment method matters

For cash or e-transfer, the buyer and seller are responsible for confirming whether payment was made, returned, or still owed. Grolo does not guarantee recovery of funds or act as escrow unless a future payment product says otherwise. For processed payments, the applicable payment provider’s refund timing and terms also apply.

## 5. Promotions

Refunds and cancellations may affect promotional eligibility. Any material promotion restrictions must be displayed before purchase and comply with law.

## 6. Disputes and support

Try to resolve the issue with the other party using Grolo messages. Contact **[support channel]** if safety, fraud, non-response, or a serious dispute is involved. We may review available records, take safety action, and request evidence, but Grolo is not automatically a party to the sale.

**Before launch, counsel should complete:** refund deadlines, payment-provider workflow, seller obligations, statutory rights by jurisdiction, high-risk goods exceptions, and chargeback handling.
