# Grolo User-Generated Content Moderation Policy

**Effective date:** [DATE]

This policy explains how Grolo handles user-generated content (UGC), including profiles, listings, photos, descriptions, messages, comments, reviews, promotions, and reports. It supplements the Terms, Community Guidelines, and Acceptable Use Policy.

## 1. What we moderate

We review content reported by users, detected through automated signals, referred by staff or partners, or identified through legal and safety processes. We may consider context, repeat behavior, account history, location, urgency, and applicable law.

## 2. Possible actions

Depending on severity and confidence, we may leave content up, request edits or verification, reduce visibility, label it, remove it, disable a feature, cancel an order, issue a warning, suspend or terminate an account, preserve evidence, or refer the matter to authorities. We may act without advance notice where urgent safety or legal concerns exist.

## 3. Priority

We prioritize imminent threats, child safety, trafficking, credible violence, dangerous goods, fraud, impersonation, privacy exposure, and illegal content. Reports should include the content location, reason, and supporting detail. Emergency situations should be reported to local emergency services first.

## 4. Appeals

If we take action against your content or account, you may appeal at **[appeal channel]** within **[X days]**, unless the notice says otherwise. Include the decision, content, and reason the action was incorrect. A trained reviewer not involved in the original decision will review where feasible. We may uphold, reverse, or modify the action and will provide an explanation appropriate to the circumstances.

## 5. Transparency and privacy

We may limit details about reporters, detection methods, investigations, or other users to protect privacy and safety. We keep moderation records for **[retention period]** or as required for safety, disputes, and law. We handle personal information under the [Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy.md).

## 6. Good-faith reporting

Do not submit knowingly false, abusive, or retaliatory reports. Repeated misuse of reporting or appeals may itself lead to enforcement. A report is not a guarantee that content will be removed or that Grolo will resolve a private dispute.
